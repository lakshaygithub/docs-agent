# Returns and Refunds

## Return window
Unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Items bought during the Boxing Week sale (December 26 to January 1) can be returned within 14 days of delivery.

## Condition of returned items
Items must be unused and have their original tags attached. Footwear must be returned in its original box.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the warehouse receives the item. Items bought as gifts are refunded as store credit instead.

## Return shipping cost
Return shipping is free for Harbourline Plus members. Everyone else pays a flat return shipping fee of $8.95, which is taken off the refund.

## Items that cannot be returned
Opened fuel canisters, personalized or engraved items, and gift cards cannot be returned.
