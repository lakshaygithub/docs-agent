# Harbourline Plus Membership

## Cost
Harbourline Plus costs $49 per year and renews automatically unless cancelled.

## Earning points
Members earn 1 point for every $1 spent before tax. Members earn double points (2 points per $1) on Harbourline-brand products.

## Using points
Every 100 points are worth $5 off a purchase. Points can only be redeemed in blocks of 100, and at least 200 points are needed to redeem.

## Points expiry
Points expire 18 months after the date they were earned.

## Member perks
Members get free return shipping and a free upgrade to express shipping on orders over $200.
