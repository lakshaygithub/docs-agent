# Stores and Price Matching

## Halifax store hours
The Halifax store is open Monday to Saturday from 9 am to 8 pm, and Sunday from 11 am to 5 pm. It is closed on December 25 and January 1.

## Price matching
We match a lower advertised price from a Canadian retailer on an identical, in-stock item, up to 14 days after purchase. We do not match marketplace sellers, clearance prices or auction sites.

## Payment methods in store
Stores accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Interac debit and Harbourline gift cards.
