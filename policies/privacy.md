# Privacy and Your Account

## What we keep
We keep order history for 7 years because tax law requires it. We never sell customers' personal data.

## Payment cards
We never store full card numbers. Payments are handled by our payment processor.

## Deleting your account
Account deletion requests are handled by the privacy team within 30 days. Accounts cannot be deleted through chat.

## Billing problems
Problems with a specific charge, such as being charged twice, are handled by the billing team, who can see payment records.
