# Shipping

## Standard shipping
Standard shipping within Canada is free on orders of $75 or more before tax. Orders under $75 pay $9.95 for standard shipping. Standard delivery takes 3 to 7 business days.

## Express shipping
Express shipping costs $19.95 and arrives in 1 to 2 business days. Express orders placed before 1 pm Eastern time ship the same day.

## Remote areas
Orders to Nunavut, the Northwest Territories and Yukon take an extra 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping is not available to these territories.

## Shipping outside Canada
We ship outside Canada to the United States only. US orders over $150 USD ship free. Customers in the US pay any duties and import taxes.

## Lost or delayed packages
If tracking has not updated for 10 business days, contact support and we will open a trace with the carrier.
