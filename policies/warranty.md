# Warranty

## Standard warranty
All Harbourline-brand products have a 1-year warranty from the date of purchase against defects in materials and workmanship. Products from other brands are covered by their maker's warranty, not by Harbourline.

## Tents and backpacks
Harbourline-brand tents and backpacks have a 5-year warranty instead of 1 year.

## What the warranty does not cover
The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, misuse, damage from UV exposure, or damage from storing an item wet or compressed.

## Making a warranty claim
To make a claim, send your order number and photos of the defect. Claims are decided within 10 business days. Approved claims are repaired, replaced or refunded as store credit, at Harbourline's choice.

## Appeals
A denied warranty claim can be appealed once. Appeals are reviewed by a warranty specialist and cannot be decided in chat.
